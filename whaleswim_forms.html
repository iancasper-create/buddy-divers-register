Travel Insurance is Compulsory

Comprehensive travel insurance is mandatory for all our WhaleSwim Adventures guests and should be taken out at the time of booking.

Your travel insurance must provide cover against personal accident, death, medical expenses and emergency repatriation with a recommended minimum coverage of US$200,000 for each of the categories of cover. It must also cover trip and flight cancellation.

We also strongly recommend it also covers curtailment, personal liability and loss of luggage and personal effects.

You must provide WhaleSwim Adventures with proof of your travel insurance within 30 days of commencement of your tour; you will not be able to join the tour without it. A copy of this policy is to be provided before this booking is confirmed.

Dietary Requirements
Please keep in mind that Tonga is a developing island nation and that we are a remote island. What is not grown there (a limited selection of vegetables and seasonal tropical fruit) is shipped in, therefore strict dietary needs are not always possible. If you have strict vegan, GF or dairy-free dietary requirements please advise us beforehand. We can provide a limited range of gluten-free items (bread and cakes) but we recommend that you bring your own GF snacks if required. For more dietary information see our Trip Preparation Bulletin.
